## 3. Detailed Day-by-Day Route Description

### Day 1 (Monday, Sept 6, 2027): Innsbruck Hbf $\rightarrow$ Oberissalm $\rightarrow$ Franz-Senn-Hütte + Alpeiner Glacier Valley Walk
- **Route**: `Oberissalm (1,742m)` $\rightarrow$ `Alpeineralm (2,040m)` $\rightarrow$ `Franz-Senn-Hütte (2,147m)` $\rightarrow$ Afternoon light-pack walk to `Kuhgschwetz / Alpeiner Bach Glacier Viewpoint (2,350m)` $\rightarrow$ `Franz-Senn-Hütte (2,147m)`
- **Distance**: `8.5 km` (`4.5 km` morning ascent to hut + `4.0 km` afternoon glacier valley walk)
- **Elevation**: `+610 m` ascent (`+410 m` morning with luggage on lift + `+200 m` afternoon light walk) | `-210 m` descent | **Max Elevation: `2,350 m`**
- **Walking Time & Grade**: `3.5 hrs` total | **T2 (Easy-Moderate Alpine Trail)**
- **Morning Transit from Innsbruck**: Take **Bus 590** (`40 mins` from Innsbruck Hbf to Neustift) + pre-booked **Taxi Schöpf / Schwab shuttle** (`20 mins`) up the Oberbergtal to **Oberissalm (`1,742 m`)**. Load heavy backpacks onto the **Franz-Senn-Hütte material cableway** at Oberissalm so you hike up with daypacks only!
- **Overnight Hut**: **Franz-Senn-Hütte (`2,147 m`)** — ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4.5/5 Comfort). Flagship DAV refuge with ~70 beds in 2–6 bed rooms (`Zimmer`), hot showers, spacious wood-paneled dining rooms, Wi-Fi, and drying room.

### Day 2 (Tuesday, Sept 7, 2027): Franz-Senn-Hütte $\rightarrow$ Schrimmennieder Pass $\rightarrow$ Neue Regensburger Hütte
- **Route**: `Franz-Senn-Hütte (2,147m)` $\rightarrow$ `Platzengrube (2,420m)` $\rightarrow$ `Schrimmennieder Pass (2,714m)` $\rightarrow$ `Neue Regensburger Hütte (2,286m)`
- **Distance**: `9.5 km`
- **Elevation**: `+760 m` ascent | `-620 m` descent | **Max Elevation: `2,714 m`**
- **Walking Time & Grade**: `4.0 – 4.5 hrs` | **T3 (Mountain Trail, short cable-protected steps near pass)**
- **Trail Highlights**: High panoramic traverse above the Oberbergtal before ascending to the **Schrimmennieder (`2,714 m`)** saddle. Spectacular views of the *Ruderhofspitze (3,474 m)* and *Hochmoosferner* glacier, followed by a scenic descent into the Falbeson high valley.
- **Overnight Hut**: **Neue Regensburger Hütte (`2,286 m`)** — ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5 Comfort). Recently rebuilt with a modern timber hotel-like wing offering 2–6 bed rooms, modern washrooms/hot showers, famous Tyrolean & gourmet vegetarian kitchen, and a sun terrace perched right beside a roaring waterfall.

### Day 3 (Wednesday, Sept 8, 2027): Neue Regensburger Hütte $\rightarrow$ Hohes Moos $\rightarrow$ Grawagrubennieder Pass $\rightarrow$ Dresdner Hütte
- **Route**: `Neue Regensburger Hütte (2,286m)` $\rightarrow$ `Hohes Moos (2,450m)` $\rightarrow$ `Grawagrubennieder Pass (2,880m)` $\rightarrow$ `Mutterberger See (2,483m)` $\rightarrow$ `Dresdner Hütte (2,308m)`
- **Distance**: `11.5 km`
- **Elevation**: `+880 m` ascent | `-860 m` descent | **Max Elevation: `2,880 m` (Highest Pass of the Trek)**
- **Walking Time & Grade**: `5.0 – 5.5 hrs` | **T3+ (High-Alpine Trail over granite blocks/moraine, steel cables on pass)**
- **Trail Highlights**: Walk through the lush glacial basin of *Hohes Moos*, then climb past the *Grawawandferner* to cross **Grawagrubennieder (`2,880 m`)**, where the giant Stubai Glacier massif opens up right in front of you. Pause at the mirror-like **Mutterberger See (`2,483 m`)** before descending to *Dresdner Hütte*.
- **Optional Afternoon Activity**: Anyone with extra energy can walk 15 minutes from the hut to climb the **Fernau Klettersteig (Via Ferrata C/D)** or walk up **Egesengrat (`2,631 m`)**, while everyone else enjoys the hut's **sauna & infrared cabin**.
- **Overnight Hut**: **Dresdner Hütte (`2,308 m`)** — ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5 Comfort). Highest comfort on the trail (~90 beds in multi-bed rooms, many with private shower/WC, sauna, huge terrace, directly at Fernau mid-station of Stubaier Gletscherbahn).

### Day 4 (Thursday, Sept 9, 2027): Dresdner Hütte $\rightarrow$ Peiljoch Pass $\rightarrow$ Blaue Lacke $\rightarrow$ Sulzenauhütte
- **Route**: `Dresdner Hütte (2,308m)` $\rightarrow$ `Peiljoch Pass (2,676m)` *(or optional Großer Trögler 2,902m)* $\rightarrow$ `Blaue Lacke (2,290m)` $\rightarrow$ `Sulzenauhütte (2,191m)`
- **Distance**: `7.5 km` (`5.5 km` direct hut-to-hut + `2.0 km` exploration around Blaue Lacke & glacier tongue)
- **Elevation**: `+450 m` ascent | `-570 m` descent | **Max Elevation: `2,676 m`**
- **Walking Time & Grade**: `3.5 – 4.0 hrs` | **T3 (Cable-secured ascent over Peiljoch)**
- **Trail Highlights**: A shorter, deeply scenic glacier day! Cross the **Peiljoch (`2,676 m`)** (stronger climbers can branch right to the summit cross of **Großer Trögler `2,902 m`** and rejoin the group 30 minutes later at the lake) to reach the turquoise waters of **Blaue Lacke (`2,290 m`)**. Descend into the green amphitheater of **Sulzenauhütte** facing the *Sulzenauferner* icefall.
- **Overnight Hut**: **Sulzenauhütte (`2,191 m`)** — ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4.5/5 Comfort). Modernized refuge with 4–6 bed family/group rooms (`Zimmerlager`), hot showers, drying room, and a material cableway down to the valley.

### Day 5 (Friday, Sept 10, 2027): Sulzenauhütte $\rightarrow$ Grawa Waterfall $\rightarrow$ WildeWasserWeg River Gorge $\rightarrow$ Falbeson $\rightarrow$ Innsbruck
- **Route**: `Sulzenauhütte (2,191m)` *(send heavy backpacks down on the Sulzenau material lift if desired!)* $\rightarrow$ `Sulzenaualm (1,857m)` $\rightarrow$ `Grawa Waterfall & Grawa Alm (1,530m)` $\rightarrow$ `Ruetz Katarakt Gorge Trail (1,380m)` $\rightarrow$ `Falbeson / Ranalt Bus Stop (1,220m)`
- **Distance**: `10.5 km` *(or exit early at `Grawa Alm` at `5.5 km` where Bus 590 also stops every 30 mins!)*
- **Elevation**: `+120 m` ascent | `-1,090 m` descent | **Max Elevation: `2,191 m`**
- **Walking Time & Grade**: `3.5 – 4.0 hrs` (`2.0 hrs` if stopping at Grawa Alm) | **T2 (Well-built waterfall & gorge trail)**
- **Trail Highlights**: Follow the **WildeWasserWeg** down alongside thundering glacial cascades through the *Sulzenaualm* basin to the monumental **Grawa Waterfall (`1,530 m`)**—the widest waterfall in the Eastern Alps, with wooden mist platforms. Continue through the dramatic *Ruetz Katarakt* river canyon to **Falbeson (`1,220 m`)** and board **Bus 590** (`50 mins`) straight back to **Innsbruck Hbf** for your Friday celebratory dinner.
