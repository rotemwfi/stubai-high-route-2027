## 2. Stage-by-Stage Distances, Elevations & Technical Difficulty

### Valley Entry / Exit Points (Clockwise & Counter-Clockwise)
- **Schlick 2000 (Fulpmes) via Kreuzjochbahn Cable Car (2,136 m) $\leftrightarrow$ Starkenburger Hütte (2,237 m)**:
  - Distance: `3.5 km` | Ascent: `+220 m` | Descent: `-120 m` | Time: `1.5 hrs` | Difficulty: **T2 (Red / Easy-Moderate)**
  - *Optional Peak on the way*: **Hoher Burgstall (2,611 m)** — Sir Edmund Hillary's first alpine peak! (`+480 m`, `2.5 hrs total`).
- **Oberissalm Hut Taxi (1,742 m) $\leftrightarrow$ Franz-Senn-Hütte (2,147 m)**:
  - Distance: `4.5 km` | Ascent: `+410 m` | Descent: `-10 m` | Time: `1.5 hrs` | Difficulty: **T2 (Easy-Moderate)** (Luggage can ride the material lift from Oberissalm!).
- **Falbeson (1,220 m) $\leftrightarrow$ Neue Regensburger Hütte (2,286 m)**:
  - Distance: `7.0 km` | Ascent: `+1,070 m` | Time: `3.0–3.5 hrs` | Difficulty: **T2/T3** (Steep valley climb past Grawa Waterfall; no cable car, though luggage lift is available from Ochsenhütte).
- **Mutterbergalm (1,720 m) via Stubaier Gletscherbahn Cable Car $\leftrightarrow$ Dresdner Hütte (2,308 m)**:
  - Distance: `0.3 km` | Ascent: `+10 m` | Time: `5 mins` from Fernau mid-station!
  - *Or walk down/up via WildeGrube*: `4.5 km`, `-590 m`.
- **Grawa Alm (1,530 m) $\leftrightarrow$ Sulzenauhütte (2,191 m)**:
  - Distance: `5.5 km` | Ascent: `+660 m` | Time: `2.5 hrs` | Difficulty: **T2** (Spectacular WildeWasserWeg trail alongside Grawa Waterfall & Sulzenaufall).
- **Bichlhof / Neder (1,000 m) or Bärenbad (1,250 m) $\leftrightarrow$ Nürnberger Hütte (2,297 m)**:
  - Distance: `6.5 km` | Ascent: `+1,050 m` | Time: `3.0 hrs` | Difficulty: **T2**.
- **Elferbahnen Cable Car Top Station (1,790 m) + Panorama Trail $\leftrightarrow$ Innsbrucker Hütte (2,369 m)**:
  - Distance: `7.5 km` | Ascent: `+680 m` | Descent: `-100 m` | Time: `3.0 hrs` | Difficulty: **T2/T3** (Scenic high-level Pinnisjoch traverse; skips 800m of valley climb!).
- **Innsbrucker Hütte (2,369 m) $\rightarrow$ Karalm (1,747 m) + Hut Taxi to Neustift (994 m)**:
  - Hike down to Karalm: `3.2 km` | Descent: `-620 m` | Time: `1.5 hrs` | Then **Karalm Shuttle Taxi** directly down through breathtaking Pinnistal to Neustift / Elferlifte (`15 mins`).

### Main High-Trail Stages (Between Huts)

1. **Starkenburger Hütte (2,237 m) $\leftrightarrow$ Franz-Senn-Hütte (2,147 m)** (via Seducker Hochalm)
   - Distance: `15.0 km` | Ascent: `+800 m` | Descent: `-890 m` | Time: `6.0–7.0 hrs` | Grade: **T3 (Black/Red)**
   - *Highlights*: Panorama below the Schlicker Seespitze, traversing above Oberbergtal. Long horizontal stage.

2. **Franz-Senn-Hütte (2,147 m) $\leftrightarrow$ Neue Regensburger Hütte (2,286 m)** (via Schrimmennieder 2,714 m)
   - Distance: `9.5 km` | Ascent: `+760 m` | Descent: `-620 m` | Time: `4.0–4.5 hrs` | Grade: **T3 (Black)**
   - *Highlights*: Classic pass crossing over **Schrimmennieder (2,714 m)**, views of Ruderhofspitze and Hochmoosferner.

3. **Neue Regensburger Hütte (2,286 m) $\leftrightarrow$ Dresdner Hütte (2,308 m)** (via Grawagrubennieder 2,880 m)
   - Distance: `11.5 km` | Ascent: `+880 m` | Descent: `-860 m` | Time: `5.5–6.0 hrs` | Grade: **T3+ (Black)**
   - *Highlights*: High lake plateau, crossing the highest standard pass on the trail (**Grawagrubennieder 2,880 m**), dramatic views of Stubaier Gletscher and Mutterberger See.

4. **Dresdner Hütte (2,308 m) $\leftrightarrow$ Sulzenauhütte (2,191 m)** (via Peiljoch 2,676 m)
   - Distance: `5.0 km` (direct) or `7.0 km` (with Großer Trögler 2,902 m + Blaue Lacke)
   - Direct Peiljoch: Ascent `+450 m` | Descent `-570 m` | Time: `2.5–3.0 hrs` | Grade: **T3 (Black, cable-secured over Peiljoch)**
   - Via **Großer Trögler (2,902 m)** summit + **Blaue Lacke (2,290 m)**: Ascent `+650 m` | Descent `-770 m` | Time: `4.0–4.5 hrs` | Grade: **T3+ (Black)**.
   - *Highlights*: Turquoise glacial lakes (**Blaue Lacke**), close-up view of Sulzenauferner glacier, summit cross of Großer Trögler.

5. **Sulzenauhütte (2,191 m) $\leftrightarrow$ Nürnberger Hütte (2,297 m)** (via Niederl 2,629 m or Mairspitze 2,781 m)
   - Distance: `6.5 km` | Ascent: `+650 m` | Descent: `-540 m` | Time: `3.5–4.0 hrs` (via Niederl) or `4.5–5.0 hrs` (via Mairspitze 2,781 m, `+780 m / -670 m`) | Grade: **T3 (Black)**
   - *Highlights*: Grünausee (emerald alpine lake), Wilder Freiger glacier backdrop, optional panoramic peak **Mairspitze (2,781 m)** with cable-assisted summit ridge.

6. **Nürnberger Hütte (2,297 m) $\leftrightarrow$ Bremer Hütte (2,411 m)** (via Simmingjöchl 2,754 m)
   - Distance: `8.5 km` | Ascent: `+680 m` | Descent: `-570 m` | Time: `4.0–4.5 hrs` | Grade: **T3+ (Black)**
   - *Highlights*: "Paradise" glacial valley (Paradies), glacier-polished granite slabs (Gletscherschliff), Simmingsee, crossing Simmingjöchl (2,754 m), Lauterer See right by Bremer Hütte.

7. **Bremer Hütte (2,411 m) $\leftrightarrow$ Innsbrucker Hütte (2,369 m)** (via Pramarnspitze / Sendesgrat)
   - Distance: `9.5 km` | Ascent: `+880 m` | Descent: `-920 m` | Time: `6.0–7.0 hrs` | Grade: **T4 / Difficult Black (Exposed, fixed steel cables, ladders/steep chimneys)**
   - *Highlights*: Widely considered the **"Queen Stage"** of the Stubai High Trail—wild, rugged, three valleys/ridges, fixed ropes & iron pins up the Pramarnspitze shoulder, arriving right below the towering pyramid of **Habicht (3,277 m)** and the Dolomite-like **Kalkkögel**.

## 3. Must-See Peaks & Via Ferrata (Klettersteige) Along the Trail

1. **Fernau Klettersteig (Via Ferrata C/D) — at Dresdner Hütte (2,308 m)**
   - **Location**: 15 mins walk from Dresdner Hütte / Fernau mid-station on the Egesengrat (2,631 m).
   - **Stats**: `+320 m` vertical, ~1.5–2.0 hours on the Ferrata (3 hrs round-trip).
   - **Character**: Fantastic, sunny granite ridge Via Ferrata with suspension bridge and panoramic glacier views. Next to it is a scenic hiking trail up the Egesengrat so non-Ferrata hikers can meet the climbers at the top!
   - **Gear Rental**: Via Ferrata sets + helmets can be rented directly at Dresdner Hütte / Gletscherbahn!

2. **Habicht (3,277 m) — Flagship 3,000m+ Peak from Innsbrucker Hütte (2,369 m)**
   - **Location**: Starts right behind Innsbrucker Hütte.
   - **Stats**: `5.5 km` round-trip | Ascent: `+910 m` | Descent: `-910 m` | Time: `4.5–5.5 hrs` round-trip | Grade: **T3+/T4 (Black alpine climb with steel cable sections; ice-free in early Sept on the normal route)**.
   - **Character**: The monarch of the eastern Stubai Alps—once thought in the 19th century to be the highest peak in Tyrol. Unbelievable 360° views from the Dolomites to the Zugspitze.

3. **Ilmspitze Klettersteig (Via Ferrata C/D) — at Innsbrucker Hütte (2,369 m)**
   - **Location**: Starts 20 minutes from Innsbrucker Hütte, climbing the dramatic limestone pinnacle of **Ilmspitze (2,692 m)**.
   - **Stats**: `+350 m` ascent | Time: `3.0–3.5 hrs` round-trip | Grade: **Via Ferrata C/D**.
   - **Character**: Exquisite Dolomite-style limestone spire right opposite the metamorphic granite of Habicht, featuring a spectacular Nepal rope bridge!

4. **Großer Trögler (2,902 m) & Mairspitze (2,781 m) — between Dresdner, Sulzenau & Nürnberger Hütte**
   - **Character**: Two almost-3,000m panoramic peaks that sit directly on optional ridge variants of the main trail between Dresdner Hütte $\rightarrow$ Sulzenauhütte (`Großer Trögler 2,902 m`) and Sulzenauhütte $\rightarrow$ Nürnberger Hütte (`Mairspitze 2,781 m`), with steel cables on the summit ridges.

5. **Wilder Freiger (3,418 m) — from Sulzenauhütte or Nürnberger Hütte**
   - **Stats**: `+1,150 m` ascent | `6.0–7.0 hrs` round-trip via Seescharte (mostly glacier-free rock ridge from Nürnberger/Sulzenau side in late summer, T4).

## 4. Transit Between Innsbruck & the Trailheads (For 10–15 Friends)

### Main Valley Line: **VVT Regiobus 590 (Innsbruck Hbf $\leftrightarrow$ Stubaital)**
- **Route**: Departs directly from **Innsbruck Hauptbahnhof (Bus terminal, Platform B/C)** every **30 minutes** all day.
- **Key Stops & Travel Times from Innsbruck Hbf**:
  1. **Fulpmes (Schlick 2000)**: ~`30 mins` (or `55 mins` via the scenic historic **Stubaitalbahn STB tram** from Innsbruck!).
  2. **Neustift im Stubaital (Elferlifte / Ortsmitte)**: ~`40 mins` by Bus 590.
  3. **Falbeson / Doadleralm**: ~`50 mins` by Bus 590.
  4. **Grawa Alm (Grawa Waterfall / Sulzenauhütte trailhead)**: ~`55 mins` by Bus 590.
  5. **Mutterbergalm (Stubaier Gletscherbahn base station / Dresdner Hütte)**: ~`65 mins` by Bus 590 (final stop!).
- **Group Tip for 10–15 Friends**:
  - For 10–15 people, you can either use **Bus 590** (with VVT Group Ticket, or free if staying 1 night in Stubaital before/after with the *Stubai Super Card*) OR pre-book two 8-passenger Mercedes Vito shuttles / a 16-seater minibus from **Taxi Schöpf / Taxi Schwab (Neustift)** (`~€90–€140` total from Innsbruck Hbf straight to the cable car base!).
  - **Extra Luggage Storage**: Any urban clothes/suitcases needed for Munich/Innsbruck can be left at your Innsbruck hotel OR transported between valley stations via luggage taxi.
