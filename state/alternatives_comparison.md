# Stubai High Trail (Stubaier Höhenweg) — Phase 1: Two Continuous 5-Day Alternatives
**Group:** Nemerim (10–15 friends, ~50 years old, Alta Via 1 veterans)  
**Trek Window:** Monday, Sept 6 – Friday, Sept 10, 2027 (5 Hiking Days / 4 Hut Nights, within TLV–MUC Flights `XOA6ZB.pdf` Sept 5–12, 2027)

---

## Alternative 1: The Glacier, Waterfall & Peak Hub Route
**Theme:** *Scenic/Moderate Alpine Pace (closest to Alta Via 1) • 3 Luxury/Modern Huts over 4 Nights • 2-Night Hub Stay at Dresdner Hütte for a Light-Pack Via Ferrata & Glacier Day*

### Continuous 5-Day Route Breakdown
- **Day 1 (Mon, 06 Sep 2027): Innsbruck $\rightarrow$ Oberissalm (Hut Taxi) $\rightarrow$ Franz-Senn-Hütte $\rightarrow$ Schrimmennieder (2,714m) $\rightarrow$ Neue Regensburger Hütte (2,286m)**
  - *Stats*: `14.0 km` | `+1,170 m` ascent (`+410 m` warm-up to Franz-Senn + `+760 m` over Schrimmennieder pass) | `-620 m` descent | `5.5–6.0 hrs` | Grade: **T3**
  - *Alternative easier start on Day 1*: Hike directly from Falbeson past the Grawa Waterfall to Neue Regensburger Hütte (`7.0 km`, `+1,070 m`, `3.5 hrs`) or do the Edelweißklettersteig at Franz-Senn and take a short afternoon stage.
- **Day 2 (Tue, 07 Sep 2027): Neue Regensburger Hütte (2,286m) $\rightarrow$ Hohes Moos $\rightarrow$ Grawagrubennieder (2,880m) $\rightarrow$ Dresdner Hütte (2,308m) [Night 1 of 2]**
  - *Stats*: `11.5 km` | `+880 m` ascent | `-860 m` descent | `5.5–6.0 hrs` | Grade: **T3+**
  - *Highlights*: Crossing the highest pass of the Stubai High Trail (**Grawagrubennieder 2,880m**), face-to-face views of the Stubai Glaciers and Mutterberger See.
- **Day 3 (Wed, 08 Sep 2027 — SLACKPACK HUB DAY!): Dresdner Hütte (2,308m) Loop — Fernau Via Ferrata (C/D) & Egesengrat (2,631m) + Glacier Ice Grotto / Top of Tyrol (3,210m) [Night 2 of 2]**
  - *Stats*: `6.0 km` (with light daypacks!) | `+450 m` / `-450 m` | `3.5–4.5 hrs` | Grade: **Optional Via Ferrata C/D or T2 Panoramic Trail**
  - *Highlights*: Leave your heavy backpacks in your rooms at Dresdner Hütte! Half or all of the group can clip into the **Fernau Klettersteig (C/D)** (gear rental available right at the hut/station) with its rope bridge and granite ridge, while anyone preferring a non-technical walk hikes the parallel panoramic trail up **Egesengrat (2,631m)** to meet on the summit! In the afternoon, optionally ride up to the **Top of Tyrol (3,210m)** viewing platform or relax in Dresdner Hütte's sauna.
- **Day 4 (Thu, 09 Sep 2027): Dresdner Hütte (2,308m) $\rightarrow$ Peiljoch (2,676m) / Großer Trögler (2,902m) $\rightarrow$ Blaue Lacke $\rightarrow$ Sulzenauhütte (2,191m)**
  - *Stats*: `7.0 km` (via Großer Trögler peak) or `5.0 km` (via Peiljoch) | `+650 m` ascent | `-770 m` descent | `4.0–4.5 hrs` | Grade: **T3 / T3+**
  - *Highlights*: Summit the panoramic **Großer Trögler (2,902m)**, picnic by the turquoise **Blaue Lacke** glacial tarn, and sleep beneath the Sulzenauferner glacier.
- **Day 5 (Fri, 10 Sep 2027): Sulzenauhütte (2,191m) $\rightarrow$ Grünausee $\rightarrow$ Mairspitze (2,781m) or Niederl (2,629m) $\rightarrow$ Nürnberger Hütte (2,297m) $\rightarrow$ Valley Descent to Bärenbad / Neder $\rightarrow$ Innsbruck**
  - *Stats*: `12.0 km` | `+650 m` ascent | `-1,590 m` descent (or exit directly from Sulzenauhütte via the spectacular **WildeWasserWeg** & **Grawa Waterfall** to Grawa Alm bus stop: `5.5 km`, `-660 m`, `2.5 hrs`!) | Grade: **T3**

### Cabin Comfort Level (Alternative 1)
- **Neue Regensburger Hütte (Night 1) — ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)**: Recently rebuilt timber annex with modern 2–6 bed rooms (*Mehrbettzimmer*), hot showers, waterfall views, and gourmet alpine dining.
- **Dresdner Hütte (Nights 2 & 3) — ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)**: Almost a 3-star mountain hotel! 90+ beds in multi-bed rooms (many with private shower/WC), sauna, huge sun terrace, and direct cable car access.
- **Sulzenauhütte (Night 4) — ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4.5/5)**: Modern rebuilt hut with 4–6 bed family/group rooms (*Zimmerlager*), hot showers, and a luggage cableway.

### Getting There & Back from Innsbruck (Alternative 1)
- **Day 1 (Innsbruck $\rightarrow$ Trail)**: Take **Bus 590** (`40 mins` from Innsbruck Hbf to Neustift) + pre-booked **Schöpf Hut Taxi** (`20 mins`) up to **Oberissalm (1,742m)** (or start at **Falbeson** / **Mutterbergalm**).
- **Day 5 (Trail $\rightarrow$ Innsbruck)**: Descend to **Bärenbad/Neder** (or **Grawa Alm** waterfall stop) and catch **Bus 590** (runs every 30 mins) straight back to **Innsbruck Hbf** (`45–55 mins`).

### Pros & Cons of Alternative 1
- **Pros**:
  - **Highest Cabin Comfort**: Best chance of getting 2–6 bed rooms (*Zimmer*) for 10–15 people every single night.
  - **2-Night Hub at Dresdner Hütte**: Gives the group a light-pack day on Wednesday for the **Fernau Via Ferrata**, plus zero stress if someone wants a rest day or if Day 3 weather is mixed.
  - **Flexible Group Splitting**: On Day 3 and Day 4, technical climbers can do the Fernau Via Ferrata and Großer Trögler (2,902m) while others take the easier Peiljoch/Egesengrat path and still eat lunch together.
- **Cons**:
  - Skips the wild eastern section (*Bremer Hütte $\rightarrow$ Innsbrucker Hütte* and *Mt. Habicht 3,277m*).

## Alternative 2: The Classic High-Alpine Traverse
**Theme:** *Challenging/Technical Alpine Trek • 4 Huts Point-to-Point (Moving Every Night) • Includes the Famous "Queen Stage" (Bremer $\rightarrow$ Innsbrucker) + Mt. Habicht (3,277m) & Ilmspitze Via Ferrata*

### Continuous 5-Day Route Breakdown
- **Day 1 (Mon, 06 Sep 2027): Innsbruck $\rightarrow$ Stubaier Gletscherbahn Cable Car to Dresdner Hütte (2,308m) $\rightarrow$ (Opt. Fernau Via Ferrata) $\rightarrow$ Großer Trögler (2,902m) $\rightarrow$ Blaue Lacke $\rightarrow$ Sulzenauhütte (2,191m)**
  - *Stats*: `8.5 km` | `+650 m` ascent | `-770 m` descent | `4.5–5.5 hrs` | Grade: **T3+ (+ Optional Via Ferrata C/D)**
  - *Highlights*: Zero boring forest climb—ride the gondola straight to 2,308m! Climb over **Großer Trögler (2,902m)** and past the turquoise **Blaue Lacke** to Sulzenauhütte.
- **Day 2 (Tue, 07 Sep 2027): Sulzenauhütte (2,191m) $\rightarrow$ Grünausee $\rightarrow$ Mairspitze Summit Ridge (2,781m) $\rightarrow$ Nürnberger Hütte (2,297m)**
  - *Stats*: `6.5 km` | `+780 m` ascent | `-670 m` descent | `4.5–5.0 hrs` | Grade: **T3+ (Cable-protected summit ridge)**
  - *Highlights*: Walk past the emerald **Grünausee**, scramble the cable-secured ridge of **Mairspitze (2,781m)** facing the Wilder Freiger glacier, and descend to Nürnberger Hütte.
- **Day 3 (Wed, 08 Sep 2027): Nürnberger Hütte (2,297m) $\rightarrow$ Paradies Glacier Slab $\rightarrow$ Simmingjöchl (2,754m) $\rightarrow$ Bremer Hütte (2,411m)**
  - *Stats*: `8.5 km` | `+680 m` ascent | `-570 m` descent | `4.0–4.5 hrs` | Grade: **T3+**
  - *Highlights*: Surreal glacier-polished granite slabs in the *Paradies* basin, crossing Simmingjöchl (2,754m), and sleeping at the highest, most atmospheric mountaineers' refuge on the trail beside the Lauterer See.
- **Day 4 (Thu, 09 Sep 2027 — THE QUEEN STAGE): Bremer Hütte (2,411m) $\rightarrow$ Pramarnspitze Shoulder (Fixed Cables) $\rightarrow$ Sendesgrat $\rightarrow$ Innsbrucker Hütte (2,369m)**
  - *Stats*: `9.5 km` | `+880 m` ascent | `-920 m` descent | `6.0–7.0 hrs` | Grade: **T4 (Challenging Black Alpine Trail — steel cables, iron pins, steep rock steps)**
  - *Highlights*: The most dramatic stage of the entire Stubai High Trail—crossing three high cirques with thrilling fixed-rope scrambling, finishing right beneath the pyramid of **Habicht (3,277m)** and facing the **Kalkkögel** ("Dolomites of North Tyrol").
- **Day 5 (Fri, 10 Sep 2027): Morning Peak / Via Ferrata at Innsbrucker Hütte $\rightarrow$ Descent to Karalm Shuttle Taxi (or Elferbahnen Cable Car) $\rightarrow$ Innsbruck**
  - *Morning Option A (3,000m+ Peak)*: Summit **Habicht (3,277m)** (`+910 m / -910 m`, `4.5–5.0 hrs` round-trip back to hut, T3+/T4 with cables).
  - *Morning Option B (Via Ferrata)*: Climb the limestone spire of **Ilmspitze Via Ferrata (C/D)** with its Nepal rope bridge (`+350 m`, `3.0 hrs` round-trip).
  - *Afternoon Valley Exit*: Hike `1.5 hrs` (`-620 m`) down to **Karalm (1,747m)** where your pre-booked **Pinnistal Shuttle Taxi** drives the group straight down to Neustift (`15 mins`), or traverse the scenic Panorama Path to the **Elferbahnen cable car** (`2.5 hrs`).

### Cabin Comfort Level (Alternative 2)
- **Sulzenauhütte (Night 1) — ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4.5/5)**: Modernized, 4–6 bed rooms available, hot showers.
- **Nürnberger Hütte (Night 2) — ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5)**: Classic large stone hut with modern annex, hot showers, great food & climbing vibe.
- **Bremer Hütte (Night 3) — ⭐⭐⭐ (3/5)**: Small, authentic, rustic high-alpine hut at 2,411m. Only ~16 beds in rooms and ~70 in classic *Matratzenlager* dorms; limited water/showers, unbeatable old-school mountaineering soul.
- **Innsbrucker Hütte (Night 4) — ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4.5/5)**: Modernized, sunny ridge hut with 2–6 bed rooms, hot showers, luggage cableway from Karalm, and incredible sunset views.

### Getting There & Back from Innsbruck (Alternative 2)
- **Day 1 (Innsbruck $\rightarrow$ Trail)**: Take **Bus 590** (`65 mins` from Innsbruck Hbf) directly to the final stop **Mutterbergalm**, then ride the **Stubaier Gletscherbahn gondola** (`10 mins`) up to **Fernau / Dresdner Hütte (2,308m)**. You start hiking already at 2,308m!
- **Day 5 (Trail $\rightarrow$ Innsbruck)**: Send heavy packs down on the **Innsbrucker Hütte material lift to Karalm**, hike down to **Karalm (1,747m)** (`1.5 hrs`), take the **Karalm Taxi** to **Neustift Elferlifte** (`15 mins`), and take **Bus 590** (`40 mins`) straight back to **Innsbruck Hbf**.

### Pros & Cons of Alternative 2
- **Pros**:
  - **Best Alpine Scenery & Thrill**: Covers the most iconic eastern half of the Stubai High Trail, including the **Bremer $\rightarrow$ Innsbrucker Queen Stage**, **Habicht (3,277m)**, and **Ilmspitze Via Ferrata**.
  - **Effortless Cable-Car & Taxi Access**: Gondola up on Day 1 (starting at 2,308m!) and Karalm Taxi + luggage lift down on Day 5.
  - **True Point-to-Point Sense of Journey**: 4 iconic huts in 4 nights across glaciers, turquoise lakes, granite slabs, and Dolomite-like limestone peaks.
- **Cons**:
  - **More Technical & Demanding**: Day 4 (Bremer $\rightarrow$ Innsbrucker) is a true black alpine stage (T4) requiring steady footing and comfort on steel-cable ledges for the whole group.
  - **Rustic Night 3 at Bremer Hütte**: The group will likely sleep in a shared *Matratzenlager* dormitory at Bremer Hütte.

## Alternative 3: The ~60 km Grand Stubai Traverse (Endurance 6–7 Huts Point-to-Point)
**Theme:** *Maximum Continuous Trail Coverage (~60 km in 5 Days) • Point-to-Point Across 6 to 7 Huts • Two Variants Depending on Cabin Comfort vs. Technicality*

Because the Stubai High Trail has two pairs of shorter adjacent stages (*Dresdner $\rightarrow$ Sulzenau $\rightarrow$ Nürnberger*), strong hikers who want **~60 km total distance** over 5 days have **two continuous ways** to achieve it:

### Variant 3A — The 60.5 km High-Comfort Western-to-Central Traverse (6 Huts Visited, 4 Luxury/Modern Overnight Huts)
*Starts at Schlick 2000 Cable Car (Fulpmes) and traverses continuously through Starkenburger $\rightarrow$ Franz-Senn $\rightarrow$ Neue Regensburger $\rightarrow$ Dresdner $\rightarrow$ Sulzenau $\rightarrow$ Nürnberger Hütte, sleeping ONLY in 4.5★–5★ huts!*

- **Day 1 (Mon, 06 Sep 2027): Innsbruck $\rightarrow$ Schlick 2000 Cable Car (Kreuzjoch 2,136m) $\rightarrow$ Starkenburger Hütte (2,237m) $\rightarrow$ Seducker Hochalm $\rightarrow$ Franz-Senn-Hütte (2,147m)**
  - *Stats*: `18.5 km` (`3.5 km` to Starkenburger + `15.0 km` high panoramic traverse to Franz-Senn) | `+1,020 m` ascent | `-1,010 m` descent | `7.0–7.5 hrs` | Grade: **T3**
  - *Highlights*: Immediate 2,136m start via cable car, views of the Dolomite-like **Kalkkögel**, and the grand panoramic balcony trail above the Oberbergtal.
- **Day 2 (Tue, 07 Sep 2027): Franz-Senn-Hütte (2,147m) $\rightarrow$ Kuhgschwetz / Schrimmennieder (2,714m) $\rightarrow$ Neue Regensburger Hütte (2,286m)**
  - *Stats*: `9.5 km` (or `11.5 km` with Hohes Moos waterfall loop) | `+760 m` ascent | `-620 m` descent | `4.5 hrs` | Grade: **T3**
  - *Highlights*: Crossing **Schrimmennieder (2,714m)** and relaxing by the waterfall terrace of the newly rebuilt *Neue Regensburger Hütte*.
- **Day 3 (Wed, 08 Sep 2027): Neue Regensburger Hütte (2,286m) $\rightarrow$ Grawagrubennieder (2,880m) $\rightarrow$ Dresdner Hütte (2,308m) + Afternoon Fernau Via Ferrata (C/D) / Egesengrat (2,631m)**
  - *Stats*: `13.5 km` (`11.5 km` main stage + `2.0 km` Egesengrat/Fernau Ferrata) | `+1,150 m` ascent | `-1,130 m` descent | `6.5–7.0 hrs` | Grade: **T3+ & Optional Via Ferrata C/D**
  - *Highlights*: Highest pass on the trail (**Grawagrubennieder 2,880m**), Mutterberger See, and late-afternoon Via Ferrata or sauna at *Dresdner Hütte*.
- **Day 4 (Thu, 09 Sep 2027): Dresdner Hütte (2,308m) $\rightarrow$ Großer Trögler (2,902m) $\rightarrow$ Blaue Lacke $\rightarrow$ Sulzenauhütte (2,191m)**
  - *Stats*: `7.0 km` | `+650 m` ascent | `-770 m` descent | `4.0–4.5 hrs` | Grade: **T3+**
  - *Highlights*: Summit cross of **Großer Trögler (2,902m)** and the iconic turquoise **Blaue Lacke** lake beneath the Sulzenauferner glacier.
- **Day 5 (Fri, 10 Sep 2027): Sulzenauhütte (2,191m) $\rightarrow$ Grünausee $\rightarrow$ Mairspitze (2,781m) $\rightarrow$ Nürnberger Hütte (2,297m — Lunch) $\rightarrow$ Valley Descent to Bärenbad / Neder $\rightarrow$ Bus 590 to Innsbruck**
  - *Stats*: `12.0 km` | `+780 m` ascent | `-1,720 m` descent | `6.0 hrs` | Grade: **T3+**
- **Total Distance (Variant 3A)**: **`56.5 km`** (Main Trail Only) | **`+3,760 m` ascent / `-4,650 m` descent** | **All 4 Overnight Huts Rated 4.5★–5★!**

### Pros & Cons of Alternative 3A (`56.5 km`)
- **Pros**:
  - **Longest Distance While Keeping 100% High-Comfort Huts (4.5★–5★)**: Traverses 6 huts (*Starkenburger $\rightarrow$ Franz-Senn $\rightarrow$ Neue Regensburger $\rightarrow$ Dresdner $\rightarrow$ Sulzenau $\rightarrow$ Nürnberger*) without needing the T4 Queen Stage or rustic Bremer Hütte dorms.
- **Cons**:
  - **Big Day 1 (`18.5 km`, `6.5–7.0 hrs`) and `+3,760 m` Total Ascent**: Still `+1,189 m` more climbing than your Alta Via 1 trek (`+2,571 m`).
