# Topographic Map Apps & GPX Upload Guide for the Stubai High Trail

## 1. How to Upload Our Generated `.gpx` Files to **AllTrails**
AllTrails accepts standard **GPX 1.1 (`.gpx`)** files via its Custom Map Builder, which syncs automatically to the AllTrails mobile app (iOS & Android) for all 10–15 friends in the group:
1. Open **[AllTrails Custom Maps](https://www.alltrails.com/explore/map/new)** on your computer browser (logged into your AllTrails account).
2. In the left sidebar, click **"Upload from file"** (or **"Import file"**).
3. Select any `.gpx` file from `state/gpx/` (e.g., `stubai_alt1_glacier_hub.gpx`, `stubai_alt2_classic_traverse.gpx`, or `stubai_alt3_habicht_hub.gpx`).
4. Click **"Save custom map"** $\rightarrow$ Your route, elevation graph, and hut waypoints will immediately appear in the **AllTrails App** under **Saved $\rightarrow$ Lists $\rightarrow$ Custom Maps** and can be shared with the WhatsApp group via a single link!

## 2. Recommended Topographic Apps for the Austrian Alps (Better than AllTrails for T3/T4 & Via Ferrata!)

While **AllTrails** is great and familiar, its default map layer in Austria often misses crucial Alpine Club details like **steel-cable protection (Seilversicherung)**, **Via Ferrata grades (A–E)**, **T2/T3/T4 SAC difficulty colors**, and **emergency bailout paths**.

We strongly recommend using one (or more) of these **4 European Alpine Apps** alongside AllTrails—all of them accept the exact same `.gpx` files generated by `topoMapExporterSKILL`:

| Rank | App / Platform | Why It's Superior for the Stubai High Trail | How to Import Our `.gpx` File |
|---|---|---|---|
| **#1 (Best Official Alpine Topo)** | **[Alpenvereinaktiv.com](https://www.alpenvereinaktiv.com) / Outdooractive** | **Official app of the Austrian & German Alpine Clubs (ÖAV / DAV)**. Uses official Austrian BEV 1:25,000 / Alpenverein cartography showing exact glacier crevasse zones, steel cables, rock ledges, and live hut status. | Web/App: Click **"Plan a Route" (Tourenplaner)** $\rightarrow$ **"Import GPX"** $\rightarrow$ Upload `stubai_alt*.gpx`. |
| **#2 (Best Free Offline Topo & Ferrata Map)** | **[Mapy.com (Mapy.cz)](https://mapy.com)** | **100% free offline vector topographic maps worldwide.** Clearly draws Via Ferrata (Klettersteige) with ladder icons, water springs, huts, and walking times between trail junctions. | Web (`mapy.com`): Click **Tools $\rightarrow$ Import GPX**, save to "My Maps" $\rightarrow$ Instant offline sync on phone! |
| **#3 (Best Group Sharing & 3D Preview)** | **[Komoot](https://www.komoot.com)** | Best UI for multi-day group tours, surface/way-type breakdown (AlpineHiking T3/T4 vs. mountain path), weather-by-hour along the trail, and 3D flyover video. | Web/App: Click **"+ Plan new" $\rightarrow$ "Import a file (GPX)"** $\rightarrow$ Choose "Mountaineering / Hiking". |
| **#4 (Best Local Austrian App)** | **[Bergfex Tours](https://www.bergfex.at)** | Austria's #1 local mountain portal with ultra-high-resolution Tyrolean orthophotos + Austrian topo maps + live Stubai webcams (Dresdner Hütte, Elfer, Schlick 2000). | App/Web: Go to **My Tours $\rightarrow$ GPX Import**. |
