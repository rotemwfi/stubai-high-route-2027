## 3. Core Design Rules for the 2 Alternatives
1. **Continuous Footpath**: Both alternatives must be 100% continuous along the Stubai High Trail network (no bus hops in the middle of the 5 hiking days).
2. **Clear Differentiation**:
   - **Alternative 1 (Scenic/Moderate + 2-Night Hub + High Comfort)**:
     - Includes a **2-night stay at a key hub hut** so the group gets one slackpack day (hiking with light daypacks only) to climb a major 3,000m peak or do a Via Ferrata while anyone wanting a lighter day can enjoy a shorter panoramic loop.
     - Uses the highest-comfort huts with the best chance of securing **2–6 bed rooms (Mehrbettzimmer / Zimmerlager)** for 10–15 people.
   - **Alternative 2 (Challenging/High-Alpine + 4 Huts Point-to-Point + Rustic/Wild)**:
     - Moves hut-to-hut every single night (**4 different huts**), covering more horizontal distance and rugged black-graded (T3+/T4) ridge/boulder terrain.
     - Includes smaller, more traditional huts (such as *Bremer Hütte*) where the group will experience classic *Matratzenlager* (shared alpine bunk rooms) and steeper cable-protected passes.
3. **Valley Access Efficiency**:
   - Always use **Bus 590** (or private minibus for 10–15 pax) from Innsbruck Hbf into the Stubai Valley, combined with **cable cars** (*Stubaier Gletscherbahn*, *Elferbahnen*, or *Schlick 2000 Kreuzjochbahn*) or **alpine hut taxis** (*Oberissalm* / *Karalm*) on Day 1 and Day 5 to skip tedious 1,000m below-treeline forest climbs.
